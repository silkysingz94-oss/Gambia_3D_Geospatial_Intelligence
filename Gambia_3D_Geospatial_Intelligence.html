Potential applications in The Gambia

  • Urban planning: Explore urban form, streets and built-up environments.
  • Infrastructure: Contextual visualisation of roads and infrastructure.
  • Coastal management: Visual interpretation of coastal landscapes.
  • Tourism: Explore towns, landmarks and tourism environments.
  • Statistical geography: Provide 3D context for census and administrative mapping.
  • Disaster risk: Support visual inspection and spatial planning.

Methodology

  • MapTiler SDK JS provides the interactive web mapping environment.
  • Satellite and hybrid styles provide imagery-based geographic context.
  • 3D terrain uses elevation data where supported by the selected map style and service.
  • Building extrusion visualises building height attributes where those data are available.
  • Camera pitch and bearing provide interactive 3D viewing perspectives.
